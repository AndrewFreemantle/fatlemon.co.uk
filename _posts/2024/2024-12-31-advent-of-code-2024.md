I started this year's Advent of Code thinking I could make videos and blog posts as by-products of attempting each day's puzzle, but the videos turned out to be a crazy amount of work to edit, and likely aren't that informative or interesting in any case!

However, summarising [each day in a short blog post]({{ site.url }}/archives/#2024) was valuable as it gave me chance to reflect on my approach.

Overall I felt this year seemed easier [than last]({{ site.url }}/aoc-2023/); though perhaps I'm more practiced at these types of challenges 😉

Well done [Eric Wastl](http://was.tl/) - 10 years of Advent of Code, long may it continue.
